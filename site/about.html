Vision

To groom and develop young kids from the slum into professional footballers and responsible leaders who will represent Nigeria and the world with pride.

Mission

To achieve this vision through hard work, discipline, moral values, and character values. "We don't just build footballers. We build MEN OF CHARACTER."

The 4Ds — Our Core Values

Discipline

On the pitch, in the classroom, and in life. We teach respect for coaches, teammates and the community.

Dedication

Consistency and commitment to growth — regular training, practice, and focus on long-term progress.

Development

Technical, tactical, physical, and mental growth: we train the whole player and support education and character formation.

Dominance

Winning with character and excellence — we aim to excel while carrying ourselves with humility and faith.
