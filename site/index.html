Who We Are

Founded: March 17, 2012 — serving Ikorodu, Lagos for 14+ years. Arise & Shine Football Academy (ASFA) grooms and develops young kids from the slum into professional footballers and responsible leaders who will represent Nigeria and the world with pride.

Read the full story →

Vision

To groom and develop young kids from the slum into professional footballers and responsible leaders who will represent Nigeria and the world with pride.

Mission

To achieve this vision through hard work, discipline, moral values, and character values. "We don't just build footballers. We build MEN OF CHARACTER."

The 4Ds — Our Core Values

Discipline

On the pitch, in the classroom, and in life.

Dedication

Consistency and commitment to growth.

Development

Technical, tactical, physical, and mental growth.

Dominance

Winning with character and excellence.
